# **MTL Korean Youth Camp**

**August 29 – 30, 2026**

The Foundation Communautaire Canadienne-Coréenne du Québec (FCCCQ), in collaboration with AKCSE McGill and KSA McGill, successfully hosted its annual mentorship event. Over the course of two days, students connected with experienced mentors through presentations, discussions, and Q\&A sessions. The event provided an opportunity for students to explore different career paths, gain insights from professionals, and reflect on their own academic and professional goals. With the support of 16 mentors, participants gained valuable perspectives and practical guidance to help navigate their next steps.

# **Eastern Canada Symposium**

**October 3rd, 2026** 

The Eastern Canada Symposium brings together students and early-career professionals from across Eastern Canada, including GTLO, Ottawa, and Montreal, to connect, share ideas, and explore emerging developments in STEM. The 2026 symposium will be held in Montreal, featuring research presentations, innovative projects, and opportunities for participants to exchange knowledge, build professional connections, and engage with the next generation of STEM research and innovation.

### **YG-YP Collaborative Christmas Party**

**December 2026** 

The YG-YP Collaborative Christmas Party brings together members of the YG and YP communities for an evening of celebration, connection, and networking. The event will provide an opportunity for young professionals and community members to come together in a relaxed and festive setting, strengthen relationships, and celebrate the achievements and connections built throughout the year. Through social activities and meaningful conversations, the gathering aims to foster a stronger sense of community and encourage continued collaboration between the YG and YP groups.

### **Research & Internship Workshop**

**January 2027** 

The Research & Internship Workshop will support students and early-career participants in preparing for upcoming summer internship opportunities. The event will feature presentations from research and industry leaders, offering insights into what organizations look for in successful internship candidates and how participants can better position themselves for these opportunities. The workshop will also include practical sessions such as CV development, career preparation, and networking, giving participants the tools, guidance, and confidence to approach the upcoming internship recruitment season.

### **Meet Your Mentor**

**March 2027** 

The annual Meet Your Mentor event, hosted in collaboration between KSA McGill and AKCSE McGill, provides students with the opportunity to connect with experienced professionals and mentors across a wide range of career fields. Through small-group discussions, networking, and open conversations, participants can learn about different academic and professional journeys in areas such as business and management, medicine and health sciences, engineering, law, technology, and more. The event is designed to encourage meaningful mentorship, broaden students’ understanding of potential career pathways, and provide practical insights into navigating their own academic and professional development.

### **CKC 2026**

**TBA** 

CKC 2026 is an annual international conference co-hosted by AKCSE and KOFST (Korean Federation of Science and Technology Societies), bringing together researchers, industry professionals, experts, and students from diverse STEM disciplines. The 2026 conference will be held in Toronto, providing a platform for participants to explore emerging technologies, showcase innovative research, and exchange ideas across scientific and technical fields. Through presentations, discussions, and networking opportunities, CKC aims to foster collaboration between the Korean and Canadian STEM communities while connecting the next generation of researchers and professionals with established leaders in the field.
